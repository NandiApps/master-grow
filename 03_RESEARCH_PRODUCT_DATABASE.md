## PART A: NUTRIENT PRODUCTS & PRICING

### 1. General Hydroponics Flora Series (Terra Aquatica - EU formulation, sold in Australia)

**Product:** Terra Aquatica TriPart® Flora Series (1 Litre bottles)  
**Supplier:** West Coast Hydroponics (wchydro.com.au)  
**Price (AUD):** $99.00 per 1L bottle × 3 bottles = **$297 AUD for complete set**

**Components:**
- TriPart® Micro (provides micro-nutrients + foundation)
- TriPart® Grow (high N for vegetative growth)
- TriPart® Bloom (high P/K for flowering)

**Key Details:**
- Available in Hard Water and Soft Water formulations
- Concentrated formula (small amounts per gallon)
- Mixing order: Micro → Grow → Bloom (prevents precipitation)
- One 1L set can treat multiple water changes (very concentrated)

**Dosage Ratios:**
- **Seedling/Cutting:** 1/4 tsp each component per 1 gallon water
- **Vegetative Growth:** 1–3 tsp per gallon (flexible)
- **Flowering:** Adjust ratios (more Bloom, less Grow)

**PPM Targets:**
- **Vegetative:** 500–1500 PPM (typical growers use 800–900)
- **Flowering:** 1000–1400 PPM (optimum 1000–1100)
- **Water change trigger:** Replace reservoir when PPM > 1150

**Cost per Tank Change (20L reservoir, typical hydro):**
- Dosing at vegetative strength (800 PPM): ~$2–5 per change
- Dosing at flowering strength (1100 PPM): ~$3–7 per change
- **Per 90-day cycle (approx 10 water changes):** ~$40–50 in nutrients

## PART B: WATER MANAGEMENT & TANK REQUIREMENTS

### Water Usage Per Plant (Hydroponic)

| Plant Size | Daily Usage | Tank Size Minimum |
|------------|------------|------------------|
| Small (seedling) | 0.5 L/day | 0.5–2 L |
| Medium (vegetative) | 1.5 L/day | 2–5 L |
| Large (flowering) | 2–2.5 L/day | 5–10 L |

**Realistic Scenario (Single 600W LED plant):**
- Seedling (weeks 0–2): 0.5 L/day → 7 L total
- Vegetative (weeks 3–5): 1.5 L/day → 22.5 L total
- Early Flower (weeks 6–8): 2 L/day → 42 L total
- Late Flower (weeks 9–10): 2.5 L/day → 35 L total
- **Total: ~107 L over 90-day cycle**

### Reservoir Management

**Top-off Schedule:**
- Check water level every 2 days
- Top off with pH-adjusted water (no nutrients)
- Prevents salt buildup

**Complete Water Change:**
- Trigger: When cumulative PPM exceeds 1150
- Frequency: Every 2–3 weeks typical (approx 3–4 changes per 90-day cycle)
- Cost: $0.10–0.20 per water change (negligible)

### Critical Parameters

| Parameter | Vegetative | Flowering | Impact of Deviation |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|-------------------|
| **pH** | 5.5–6.5 | 5.5–6.5 | >6.8: lockout; <5.5: Ca/Mg deficiency |
| **EC** | 1.3–1.7 | 1.2–2.0 | Too low: slow growth; too high: burn |
| **Water Temp** | 60–80°F | 60–80°F | >80°F: root rot risk; <60°F: slow uptake |
| **Water Change Frequency** | Every 2 weeks | Every 2 weeks | >3 weeks: salt buildup; EC creep |

## PART C: ELECTRICITY COSTS

### 600W LED Grow Light Setup

**Actual Power Draw:** ~600W (advertised "600W" often means actual 600W for LEDs)

**Daily Cost (Australia):**
- Assuming $0.30 AUD per kWh (Australian average, higher than US)
- 600W × 18 hours veg = 10.8 kWh/day = **$3.24/day**
- 600W × 12 hours flower = 7.2 kWh/day = **$2.16/day**

**90-Day Cycle Cost Breakdown:**
- Vegetative (21 days @ 18h): 227.6 kWh = $68.28
- Flowering (69 days @ 12h): 497.2 kWh = $149.16
- **Grow Light Total:** ~$217.44 AUD

**Supporting Equipment (fans, pump, heater):**
- Estimated 75% of grow light power = $163.08
- **Total Electricity:** ~$380 AUD per 90-day cycle

## PART G: KEY TAKEAWAYS FOR GAME MECHANICS

### What This Data Tells Us About Game Balance

1. **Nutrient costs are LOW** (~$50 per cycle) compared to electricity ($380).
   - **Game implication:** Electricity management, not nutrient optimization, is the real cost driver.
   - **Mechanic:** Light schedule (hours/day) should have strong cost impact.

2. **Water is essentially free** (~$5 per cycle).
   - **Game implication:** Water management is about plant health (pH, EC), not cost.
   - **Mechanic:** Water quality (pH, EC balance) affects growth rate/health, not expense.

3. **Seed cost ($80) is high relative to consumables** (~$50 nutrients + $380 electricity).
   - **Game implication:** Seed selection is a major strategic choice.
   - **Mechanic:** Higher-tier seeds command premium prices but require same nutrient investment.

4. **Cycle profitability hinges on:**
   - Strain choice (genetic yield potential)
   - Yield optimization (nutrients, light, conditions)
   - Market price (linked to seed tier)
   - **Not** on nutrient cost-cutting

5. **Break-even typically at Cycle 2–3** (after initial setup amortization).
   - **Game implication:** Early cycles are high-risk, incentivizing progression.
   - **Mechanic:** First harvest margin is tight; subsequent harvests are profitable.
